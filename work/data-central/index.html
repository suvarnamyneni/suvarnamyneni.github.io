Process

Leadership early, and a living document.

The design process in the case study engages leadership early, at a level that fits the phase. The people named are not limited to users and customers: designer, researcher, technologist, product management, leadership, editorial, legal and support. It is described as a living document, with collaboration across teams, gathering qualitative or quantitative data for the product vision.

Define users and the problem

Research into motivations, mental models, behaviors, differences and commonalities, so assumptions can be checked against pain points.

Mockups and prototype

A quick path from low to high fidelity, to check the approach against expectations and deadlines.

Build and validate

Research

Five apps, and no single place to manage a database.

The Database Manager vision is to make database tasks intuitive, efficient and compliant for BDT users: remove unnecessary work, and let people register databases, manage compliance posture, control access, search, discover and view database information, and configure job performance.

It started in design office hours, a time-boxed consultation from the UX engagement model. A freehand board covered context, problem statements and how-might-we prompts. The current journey was mapped. The PM’s low-fidelity mock and user stories were part of the kickoff. Concept mocks covered 4 main use cases.

Personas

3 of 7 BDT personas fit this project.

Of seven BDT personas, three fit Database Manager: people who mainly produce and transform data, and also consume it.

Use case

Create a database the team can run jobs on.

As a Data Central user, I want to create a new Redshift, RDS PostgreSQL or RDS MySQL database, so my team can use it for running jobs and Andes subscriptions.
